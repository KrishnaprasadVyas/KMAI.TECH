# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

High-growth founders, enterprise leaders, and cultural institutions in need of custom digital infrastructure, bespoke web experiences, or operations automation without agency bloat or intermediate account management.

## Product Purpose

KMAI is an independent digital technology studio architecting high-performance software, bespoke web experiences, and operational automation pipelines. Success means shipping production-grade digital assets that eliminate manual human error, elevate brand authority, and drive measurable organizational outcomes.

## Positioning

An end-to-end digital partner that unites high-touch visual art direction and kinetic motion choreography with hardened backend database, cloud, and automation infrastructure. Engagements are executed through focused, high-impact 4–8 week design and engineering sprints.

## Operating Context

- **Engagement Model**: Fixed-scope, high-impact 4–8 week sprints for flagship web platforms, software architectures, and automated operational pipelines.
- **Direct Engagement**: Direct partnership with founding architects and engineers (`contact@kmai.tech`); confidential NDA provided upon request; 24-hour response SLA.
- **Delivery Standards**: Lighthouse 100 performance, sub-second loads, hardware-accelerated 120Hz smooth scrolling, zero runtime warnings or errors.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Core Capabilities**:
  1. Software Architecture & Engineering (Microservices, scalable APIs, relational & realtime databases, CI/CD).
  2. Bespoke Web Design & Interactive Platforms (Editorial visual design, GPU-accelerated motion, kinetic typography, Lighthouse 100 SEO).
  3. Business & Operations Automation (CRM/ERP workflows, invoicing engines, messaging bots, automated payment pipelines).
  4. Applied AI Solutions (Custom LLM integrations, retrieval pipelines, agentic workflows, autonomous data processing).
- **Stack & Architecture**: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, Lenis.
- **Deliberate Exclusions**: No low-budget freelance commodity templates, no generic AI-slop wrappers, no multi-layered account management.

## Product Principles

1. **Craft Over Commoditization**: Every platform is treated as bespoke digital architecture; never rely on generic templates or vibe-coded clichés.
2. **End-to-End Cohesion**: Fluid visual art direction and motion must be matched by hardened backend infrastructure and clean type-safe code.
3. **Pacing and Directness**: 4–8 week focused sprints; direct engineer-to-client collaboration with zero intermediary account managers.
4. **Observable Precision**: Rigorous performance budgets, zero console errors, 100% test passing rates, and genuine client proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` across all GSAP timelines and interactive state transforms.
- Clean semantic HTML headings, high contrast text ratios (WCAG AA compliance on `#07090E`), and immediate touch-safe fallback states for cursor tracking and magnetic triggers on mobile/tablet viewports.
